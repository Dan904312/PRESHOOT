Privacy Policy

Last Updated: August 18, 2026

Welcome to PreShoot ("PreShoot", "we", "our", or "us"). This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, and protect your information when you use our website, web application, and related services.

By using PreShoot, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this Privacy Policy.

1. Information We Collect

We may collect the following types of information:

Account Information

When you create an account using Google Login, we may collect:

  • Your email address
  • Account creation/sign-up date
  • Basic profile information associated with your Google account

Creator Profile & Usage Data

To personalize your experience, PreShoot may store:

  • Creator profile information (niche, platform, aesthetic, gear)
  • Past scan history
  • Preferences and usage patterns
  • AI-generated outputs and recommendations

Operational usage metrics

After usage tracking is enabled, PreShoot records limited operational telemetry so we can operate the product, estimate AI provider cost, prevent abuse, and administer accounts. This may include:

  • Successful scan, Director, and other billable AI operation counts
  • AI provider name, model identifier, token or unit counts, and an estimated cost
  • Request identifiers and coarse metadata (for example, whether a request was streamed)
  • Account security events such as suspension and restoration
  • Administrative audit logs of operator actions
  • Transactional or operational emails if an email delivery provider is connected, including recipient, subject, send time, and delivery status

We do not store API keys, passwords, or access tokens in these logs. We do not store full Director conversation contents in the usage ledger. Prompt text is not written to the usage ledger.

If a feature is not enabled (for example, no email provider is configured), that data is not collected as a delivered message. Failed send attempts may still be logged as failed so operators are not shown a false “sent” status.

Uploaded Content

Users may upload images or other content for AI analysis and content generation purposes.

PreShoot itself does not manually access or review uploaded user content unless required for security, legal compliance, abuse prevention, or technical troubleshooting.

Payment Information

Payments and subscriptions are processed securely through Stripe. PreShoot does not store or directly access full credit card or payment information.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use collected information to:

  • Provide and operate PreShoot services
  • Generate AI-powered content ideas and recommendations
  • Personalize outputs based on your creator profile and scan history
  • Improve platform functionality and user experience
  • Process subscriptions and payments
  • Prevent abuse, fraud, or misuse of the platform
  • Maintain platform security and stability
  • Measure operational usage and estimated AI provider cost
  • Send transactional or operational emails when a delivery provider is configured
  • Investigate security incidents and administer accounts

3. AI Processing & Third-Party Providers

PreShoot uses third-party AI providers, including Anthropic, to generate responses and content suggestions.

Uploaded content and related prompts may be securely transmitted to these providers in order for features to function properly.

YouTube & CapCut creative research. PreShoot builds idea-specific research (style, niche, hook, and edit intent) to recommend YouTube references and CapCut template searches. When a YouTube Data API key is configured on the server, PreShoot may retrieve public video metadata (title, channel, thumbnail, view counts) to rank a shortlist — this metadata is not used to train models and is not stored as a permanent archive of your research. CapCut does not offer a public third-party OAuth API; if you “connect CapCut,” PreShoot only stores your connection preference and optional display name in your PreShoot profile so template research can unlock. PreShoot does not upload your photos to YouTube or CapCut. Opening a recommendation takes you to that platform under their own terms and privacy policies.

We do not control how third-party providers process data once transmitted through their systems. Users should also review the privacy policies of:

  • Anthropic
  • Stripe
  • Google
  • Vercel
  • Supabase
  • YouTube / Google
  • CapCut / Bytedance

4. Hosting & Infrastructure

PreShoot uses third-party infrastructure and hosting providers, including:

  • Vercel
  • Supabase

These providers may process and store data as necessary to operate the platform securely and reliably.

5. International Users

PreShoot may be accessed by users worldwide. By using the service, you understand that your information may be processed or stored in countries outside your own jurisdiction, including countries that may have different data protection laws.

6. Data Retention

We retain information for as long as necessary to:

  • Provide our services
  • Maintain user accounts
  • Improve platform functionality
  • Comply with legal obligations
  • Resolve disputes and enforce agreements

Operational records are intended to be retained for limited periods unless a longer period is required for security, accounting, or law:

  • AI usage events: up to 24 months
  • Administrative audit logs: up to 24 months
  • Operational email logs: up to 24 months

These periods describe product intent. Automated deletion jobs may not yet run in every environment. Users may request deletion of their account and associated data by contacting us.

This policy is written for an Australian business context and should be reviewed by qualified legal counsel. It does not claim ISO, SOC 2, GDPR certification, or other compliance marks that PreShoot has not independently obtained.
