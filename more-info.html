The landing page is the short version. This page is for people who want the workflow, Studio, Director, and how PreShoot thinks about production.

The workflow

Understand Scan Ideas Choose Studio Director Ready to film

You do not have to know filmmaking language to start. You do have to pick an idea and send it to Studio. That is the next step, every time.

Scan

A photo is the input. PreShoot reads what is in the frame, then combines it with your creator profile: niche, audience, platform, style, gear, and skill. That is why two people can scan the same counter and get different plans.

Ideas and hooks

Each idea comes with several opening hooks. None of them is “the main one.” You can select one or several, then send the idea to Studio. YouTube and CapCut references stay on the idea as supporting research. They are not the next step. Studio is.

Studio

Studio is the production. Script, shot list, assets, and status live on one production inside a project. You should always be able to see the project name, the production name, and the section you are in: Overview, Script, Shot List, and so on.

Beginners and professionals

Beginner guidance sounds like: put the phone here, film for five seconds, say this. Professional detail (lens, movement, lighting) is available when your profile says you want it. It is not dumped on everyone at once.

Director

Director is the AI partner for the current production, not a generic chat box. Ask it to improve a hook, make a plan easier to film, shorten a script, or adjust for iPhone. Less chatting. More action on this project.

Creator context

Your profile is the memory: niche, platforms, aesthetic, gear, posting rhythm. PreShoot uses it on every scan and in Studio so the plan matches what you can actually shoot this week.

Business use

Shops, studios, and teams can scan a product or a location, lock a format (advert, UGC, launch), and keep the production in Studio. Shared workspaces add roles and comments without turning the document into a chat thread.

What PreShoot does not do

  • It does not generate finished videos.
  • It is not a replacement for CapCut, Premiere, or your camera.
  • It is not a general chatbot. ChatGPT is flexible intelligence. PreShoot is production direction.

More questions

Does it remember my preferences?

Yes, in your creator profile and in the productions you save. Sign in to keep that across devices.

Which platforms can I create for?

TikTok, Reels, Shorts, YouTube longform, LinkedIn, and others you set in Platform Focus. Ideas adapt to the platform you mark as primary.

Is this only for professional creators?

No. If you can take a photo of the thing you want to film, you can use it. Professionals get more production language when they want it.
