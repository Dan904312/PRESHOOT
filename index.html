FAQ

Questions, answered quickly.

What is PreShoot?

PreShoot is an AI creative production tool. You scan a scene, get personalised video ideas, then send the one you want into Studio as a production-ready plan.

Who is it for?

Creators, small businesses, and anyone who needs to post video but does not want to start from a blank page. Beginners and working professionals both use the same product, with different levels of production detail.

Does PreShoot generate videos?

No. It does not render finished videos. It gives you the idea, hook, script, and shot list so you can film.

How is this different from ChatGPT?

A chatbot answers in a thread. PreShoot keeps creator context, then turns a chosen idea into a production you can open in Studio and Direct.

What happens when I upload a photo?

PreShoot reads the scene and combines it with your profile: niche, platform, style, and gear. You get several ideas you can actually shoot with what you have.

Do I need a professional camera?

No. Phone, camera, or mixed kits all work. Guidance changes with the skill and gear on your profile.

What is Studio and Director?

Studio is the production workspace: script, shot list, assets. Director is the AI partner for that project. You choose an idea first, then send it to Studio.

Does PreShoot remember my preferences?

Yes. Your niche, platforms, style, and gear live on your creator profile. Sign in to keep them across devices.

Can businesses use PreShoot?

Yes. Shops, studios, and teams can plan content from a product, a location, or a campaign brief, then keep the production in Studio.
